ANIME OF THE YEAR WINNERS

Here are the best and top rated anime from the past years.

AOTY 2020

Demon Slayer:Kimetsu no Yaiba

  • Release Year: April 2019
  • Studio: Ufotable
  • Genre: Adventure, Dark Arts, Fantasy
  • Seasons: 4(63 episodes)
Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba won Anime of the Year at the 2020 Crunchyroll Anime Awards. Its stunning Ufotable animation and heartfelt story of Tanjiro and Nezuko captivated fans worldwide. The win marked the start of Demon Slayer’s rise as a global cultural phenomenon.

AOTY 2021

Jujutsu Kaisen

  • Release Year: October 2020
  • Studio: MAPPA
  • Genre: Adventure, Dark Arts, Fantasy
  • Seasons: 3(59 episodes)
Jujutsu Kaisen won Anime of the Year at the 2021 Crunchyroll Anime Awards, solidifying MAPPA’s reputation for high-quality action anime. It also took home awards for Best Antagonist (Ryomen Sukuna) and Best Ending Sequence (“Lost in Paradise”). The win reflected its explosive popularity and impact as one of the standout series of 2020.

AOTY 2022

Attack on Titan:Final Season

  • Release Year: April 2013
  • Studio: Wit Studio, MAPPA
  • Genre: Adventure, Dark Arts, Post-apocalyptic
  • Seasons: 4(94 episodes)
Attack on Titan: Final Season Part 1 won Anime of the Year at the 2022 Crunchyroll Anime Awards. Its gripping narrative, shocking twists, and MAPPA’s intense animation captivated millions of fans worldwide. The award solidified Attack on Titan’s legacy as one of the most impactful anime series of its era.

AOTY 2023

Cyberpunk:Edgerunners

  • Release Year: SEPTEMBER 2022
  • Studio: TRIGGER
  • Genre: Cyberpunk
  • Seasons: 1(10 episodes)
Cyberpunk: Edgerunners won Anime of the Year by combining Studio Trigger’s explosive, neon-drenched animation with a tragic, emotionally gripping story that captivated global audiences. It transcended its video game roots through iconic character development and a viral soundtrack, revitalizing the entire franchise. Ultimately, its ability to deliver a polished, standalone narrative made it a rare crossover hit that set a new benchmark for modern anime adaptations.

AOTY 2024

Jujutsu Kaisen:Season 2

  • Release Year: October 2020
  • Studio: MAPPA
  • Genre: Adventure, Dark Arts, Fantasy
  • Seasons: 3(59 episodes)
Jujutsu Kaisen’s second win cemented its status as a visual powerhouse, though many felt it prioritized hype and animation over deeper storytelling. The victory sparked debate, as it beat out critically acclaimed series like Vinland Saga in what some called a popularity contest. Ultimately, it proved that massive shonen hits currently dominate the awards landscape.

AOTY 2025

Solo Levelling: Season 2

  • Release Year: March 2024
  • Studio: A-1 Pictures
  • Genre: Action, Fantasy, Adventure
  • Seasons: 2(25 episodes)
Solo Leveling's 2025 win was a historic breakthrough, marking the first time a Korean manhwa adaptation claimed the top prize. While its "zero-to-hero" action and A-1 Pictures' production were massive hits, critics argued it relied on recency bias to beat more artistic masterpieces like Frieren. It essentially proved that high-octane power fantasies still hold the most sway with the global voting public.
